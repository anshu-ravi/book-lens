How it works

Getting started

Step 1 — Library
Create a series

Every book lives inside a series. For standalone novels, use the same name for both the series and the book. Tap + New Series in the Library tab to begin.

Step 2 — Upload
Add your books

Go to the Upload tab, select your series, enter the book's position in the series, then drop in your EPUB file. Indexing takes about a minute. You can upload books in any order.

Step 3 — Ask
Ask anything

Once a book is indexed, head to the Ask tab. Select your series and ask about characters, plot details, themes, or relationships.

Good to know
  • BookLens is spoiler-safe — it only draws on content up to where you've read. Mark a book as Reading or Completed in the Library to enable this.
  • Keep your chapter progress up to date in the Library. The spoiler filter uses it across the whole app.
  • Only EPUB files are supported.
